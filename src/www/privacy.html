Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Inlet is a Discord bot that verifies members before giving them access to a server. This policy explains what we collect to do that, and what we don't.

What we collect

When a member verifies, or an admin configures the bot, we store:

  • Server configuration: your server's ID, the verified role ID, and the redirect channel ID.
  • Authorization records: the server ID, your Discord user ID, whether the bot granted a role, and a timestamp.
  • A device fingerprint: a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from request headers (browser, language, and similar). We can't reverse it into your browser details; it is used to spot repeated or abusive verification attempts.

What we use but don't store

  • Discord authorization: verifying uses Discord's role_connections.write scope. The access token is used for that request and is not saved to our database.
  • Your roles in the server: read at verification time to decide whether the verified role needs to be added.
  • CAPTCHA: we use ALTCHA, a proof-of-work challenge that runs on our own server. It doesn't use cookies or send data to a third-party CAPTCHA provider.

Linked role metadata

After you verify, we update your Discord profile's role connection with how many times you've verified and when you last did. This is visible to you and to servers that use Discord linked roles.

How we use data

Only to operate the bot: granting the verified role, redirecting you after verification, showing your verification count, and preventing abuse. We don't sell data, show ads, or build profiles.

Who we share it with

We don't share data with anyone for their own purposes. It is processed by infrastructure providers needed to run the service, namely Discord (the platform itself), Cloudflare (hosting), and our database host. Server admins don't get access to authorization records through the bot.

Retention and deletion

We keep data for as long as it's needed to run the bot. Removing the bot from your server stops new data from being collected for it. To request deletion of your data, or of your server's data, open an issue on GitHub (please don't post private information publicly).

Children

Inlet is only for use on Discord, which requires users to meet its minimum age. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the bot means you accept the updated policy.
