The service

Inlet is a Discord bot that lets servers require members to complete a short verification before receiving a role. It is provided free of charge and may change at any time.

  • Server admins are responsible for how they configure Inlet, including the roles and channels it manages, and for telling their members how verification works.

Permissions and setup

Setting up Inlet changes your server: it removes View Channel from @everyone, can create a verified role and a verification channel, and assigns the role to members who verify. Review these changes before you run setup. Only members with the required permissions can run setup commands.

Verification isn't a guarantee

Verification reduces automated and low-effort abuse, but it can't guarantee that a member is human, trustworthy, or who they claim to be. You're responsible for moderating your own server.
